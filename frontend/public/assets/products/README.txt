Real JK Velvet photography goes here.

Drop one JPG per fabric, named by its product slug, e.g.:
  holland-velvet.jpg
  micro-11000-falcon.jpg
  coco-velvet.jpg

Then tell the builder "photos are uploaded" — one line per product in
src/data/catalog.js gets a `photo: '<slug>.jpg'` entry and the site
instantly swaps placeholders for the real fabric.
